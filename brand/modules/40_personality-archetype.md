## Synthesis

### Archetype

**Sage (primary) + Creator (secondary).** The Sage explains exactly how it works and what it doesn't do. The Creator hands over a well-made tool and steps back: "here it is, I believe in it, do whatever you want."

Rejected: Outlaw ("I know better"). Everyman was not chosen. Playful nerd energy survives only as a rare wink.

### Always / never

- **Always:** reliable, secure, elegant.
- **Never:** "I know better" (condescending, preachy or superior).

"Secure" is a character trait, not a copy claim. Public copy names specific properties with tests behind them (ADR-0031) and never says "secure" or "military-grade" on its own.

### Aaker personality dimensions

| Dimension | Level | Expression |
|---|---|---|
| Competence (reliable, intelligent) | High | Exact specs, tested claims, honest limits |
| Sincerity (honest, down-to-earth) | High | Says what the relay sees, and never oversells |
| Sophistication (elegant) | Medium-high | Restraint, precision and craft, not luxury |
| Ruggedness (tough) | Medium | Survives offline, restarts and hostile relays, shown as evidence and not swagger |
| Excitement (daring, spirited) | Low | Interest comes from precision, not hype |

### Personality in one line (candidates)

A. "A quiet expert who hands you a perfect tool and trusts you with it."
B. "Precise, honest, generous: the primitive you'd have built yourself if you had a year."
C. "It explains itself exactly, then gets out of your way."

### Visual implications (feeds 90_SYNTHESIS and the docs prototype)

- **Plain and precise:** a monochrome base, with at most one accent colour used for meaning (state, emphasis), never for decoration.
- **Elegant:** restraint, generous whitespace, strict alignment, and a typographic hierarchy doing the work.
- **Primitive:** square corners and the blinking cursor as a signature of "developer, terminal, live".
- **Reliable:** nothing flashy or animated without purpose. Gradients only if they encode something.
- **Not "I know better":** no dark-mode hacker clichés, no skulls or padlocks, no fear imagery.
