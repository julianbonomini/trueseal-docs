# Roadmap & Research

TrueSeal is a Developer Preview. It delivers end-to-end encrypted messages between the devices of a group, through a relay that can't read or forge them. What 1.0 needs is a commitment. The research direction after it is only an intent, and I'd rather keep the two apart.

## 1.0

TrueSeal reaches 1.0 when four fixed criteria hold, and it has no target date. [Developer preview](/docs/overview/developer-preview#what-must-be-true-before-10) lists the criteria and what 1.0 doesn't require.

## Research direction

Today every member of a group receives every message. The direction I want to explore is a device receiving only the messages it holds a capability for.

The addressed encryption TrueSeal already uses points at how. A message is sealed to a public key, and only the holder of the private key can open it. If a message could be sealed to a key for one object or one topic instead of a device, holding that key would be the permission to read it, and no server would decide who gets what.

Sealing to such a key is the easy part. The open question is how a device gets a new key of that kind without a full pairing and without someone in the middle handing keys out, while the relay still learns nothing new. Pairing hands out keys for a whole device. I don't know yet how to do it per object or per topic, and I'm not promising that it will ship.
