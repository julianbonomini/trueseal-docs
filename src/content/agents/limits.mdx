# Limits

Values follow the accepted ADRs for the Developer Preview.

## Client limits

<FactTable group="clientLimits" />

- `send()` refuses a `Sync` body over the Protocol Size Limit with `payloadTooLarge{max}`, and the body never enters the outbox.
- A caller may pass a lower `maxPayloadBytes` to `TrueSeal.open`; a higher value is clamped to the Protocol Size Limit.
- `accept(request)` fails with `groupFull{max}` when admitting would exceed the Maximum Group Size.

## Relay limits

<FactTable group="relayLimits" />

- The Operator can change every relay value, but can only lower the Relay Size Limit and the TTL.
- The relay refuses to start with a Relay Size Limit above the Protocol Size Limit, or a TTL above <Fact id="relayTtlMax" />.
- Every relay limit is keyed on the recipient Inbox, the device key or a global count, never on an IP address. Per-IP limits belong at the Operator's firewall or reverse proxy.
- Anyone who knows a device's public key can fill that device's Inbox quota and delay its delivery.
