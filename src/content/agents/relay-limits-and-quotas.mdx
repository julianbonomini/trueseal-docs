# Relay limits and quotas

This page states the relay's limits in the Developer Preview as trueseal-relay ADR-0012 decides them, with the size limit of trueseal-sync ADR-0025 and the TTL ceiling of ADR-0034. The client's response to each refusal is on [Delivery issues](/agents/delivery-issues).

## Keying

- Push Sessions carry no sender identity, so no limit is keyed on a sender.
- Every limit is keyed on the recipient Inbox, the Device key, or a global count.
- No limit reads, stores or is keyed on an IP address. Per-IP limiting belongs at the Operator's firewall or reverse proxy.

- Refusal codes carry no content.
- On a temporary refusal the client keeps the outbox entry and retries with backoff. No delivery issue is raised.
- On `too large` the client removes the outbox entry and raises `sendFailed(messageId, tooLarge)`.

The client starts heartbeats. The relay does not.

## Retention

- A Blob stays in its Inbox until the recipient sends DeliverAck, which the client sends after its handler finishes (trueseal-sync ADR-0026), or until the TTL.
- The reaper deletes expired Blobs every hour by default.
- A reaped Blob is reported to neither device.

## Changing limits

- The Operator can change every value.
- The envelope size limit (the Relay Size Limit) and the TTL can only be lowered.
- The relay refuses to start with a size limit above the Protocol Size Limit.
- The relay refuses to start with a TTL above <Fact id="relayTtlMax" />: the Replay Window (<Fact id="replayWindow" />) minus the client Outbox expiry (<Fact id="outboxExpiry" />), so a message pushed on its last outbox day still arrives inside the Replay Window.
- An app using a relay with a lower Relay Size Limit passes a matching lower `maxPayloadBytes` to `open()`. `maxPayloadBytes` counts the body; the Relay Size Limit counts the sealed envelope.

## Accepted attack

Anyone who knows a Device's public key can use up that Device's quota and delay its delivery. This is a targeted availability attack. The relay does not prevent it. Confidentiality and integrity of the queued Blobs are unaffected.
